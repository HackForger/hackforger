# SynNovator Developer Hub: Quick Start Guide

Welcome to **SynNovator** — the world's first Git-native developer co-creation platform. Here, a Git repository is not just a code container; it is your sole identity credential for joining a **Hackathon**, claiming a **Bounty**, and receiving a **Grant**.

---

## Chapter 1: Core Concept — The Credits Economy

**Why Credits?**
SynNovator runs on a unified Credits ledger. To quantify your contributions and provide tangible rewards, all prizes you earn through competitions and bounties are deposited into your personal account as Credits.

* **Spending and redeeming**: Credits can be exchanged in the official store for AI compute quotas (for ongoing development), GitHub Copilot keys, or a cash settlement request.
* **Checking your balance**: Click the `Credits` icon in the top navigation bar to view your balance and a full transaction history (e.g., `hackathon_prize` or `escrow_release`).

## Chapter 2: Hackathon Co-creation Marathon

### 2.1 Registration & Bind
> **Why this works this way**: To ensure continuity of project development, the competition follows a one-contest-one-repo rule. Registration is not just an identity check — it locks your entry slot to a specific repository.

1. **Open the page**: Click "Register" on the `Explore -> Hackathons` page.
2. **Choose a repo**: Select a repository you own from the dropdown. If you don't have one, click `+ Create New Repository`.
3. **Automatic identity detection**: Selecting a personal repository registers you as an **individual participant**; selecting an Organization repository registers you as a **team participant**.
4. **Complete registration**: Fill in your project title, description, and Track information, then submit.
   * **Warning**: Your competition repository is locked once selected in Wave 1 and cannot be changed for the rest of the event.

### 2.2 Development
> **Why this works this way**: The essence of a Git-native competition is "process as proof."

* **Push code**: You can push code and collaborate normally within your locked repository.
* **Iterate your submission**: In subsequent Waves, click "Submit Project." Because your repo is already locked, you only need to update your latest project concept or Demo URL — the system automatically links the current state of your repository.

### 2.3 Code-Based Judging
> **Why this works this way**: To eliminate the disconnect between documentation and code, and bring judging back to its technical roots.

* **AI review**: Scoring is based on your repository content and the results of evaluation tasks (reviewer comments).
* **Judge scoring**: Judges will review your repository or PR interface directly.
* **Scoring criteria**: Weighted scoring based on "Innovation," "Technical Quality," and "Presentation."

## Chapter 3: Bounty Handbook

> **Why this works this way**: To give developers an immediate path to monetization while helping organizers resolve specific technical issues.

* **Exclusive mode**: Funds are placed in escrow upfront. Once you apply and are approved, you become the sole assignee, and the escrowed funds are automatically released to you when the PR is merged.
* **Competitive mode**: Multiple hackers compete; prizes are shared from the pool according to PR quality ranking.
* **Automatic protection**: If a bounty expires or is cancelled, escrowed funds are automatically returned to the issuer, protecting both parties.

---

## Chapter 4: AI Agent Support

> **Why this works this way**: SynNovator is committed to building a human-AI co-creation ecosystem that treats AI agents as independent contributors.

* **PAT authentication**: Go to `Settings -> Applications -> Generate Personal Access Token (PAT)` to allow your AI Agent to make API calls on your behalf.
* **Automation capabilities**: Agents can automatically scan Bounty requirements, fork repositories, submit PRs, and monitor account balances.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: I want to participate as a team — how do I do that?**
A: First create an "Organization" on the platform, add your teammates to it, then select a repository under that organization when you register.

**Q: How do I update my competition proposal?**
A: Every Wave has a submission window. Update your project description in the corresponding Wave submission form and the system will automatically sync the latest content.

**Q: Can I change my repository after registration?**
A: No. Your competition repository is locked once selected in Wave 1. Please confirm your choice carefully at registration time.

**Q: Can I team up with an AI teammate?**
A: Absolutely. You can add your AI Agent ID as a repository collaborator to achieve true human-AI co-creation.
